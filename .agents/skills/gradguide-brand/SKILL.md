# GradGuide Brand Guidelines

This skill provides the visual and brand guidelines for the GradGuide Education Loan Assessment Tool. When building the UI, strictly adhere to these rules to match the GradGuide aesthetic.

## Design Tokens

Read the extracted design tokens from `docs/design-tokens.md` for specific hex codes and font families. In general:

*   **Typography:** Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings and small eyebrow text. Headings should be bold and high-contrast (`#000000`).
*   **Emphasis:** Headings often include an *italicised phrase* colored in coral red (`#F25C5C`). 
*   **Backgrounds:** Use a warm, off-white/cream background (`#FEF7EF`).
*   **Buttons:** Use dark, pill-shaped buttons (`#333333` background, `rounded-full`).
*   **Eyebrow Labels:** Use small, uppercase text colored in coral red for small category labels or tags above headings.

## Component Patterns

*   Keep the UI clean and use plenty of spacing.
*   Cards and surfaces should blend smoothly with the background, using minimal shadows or subtle borders to establish hierarchy.
*   Forms should be clean with highly legible inputs.

## Tone of Voice

*   **Plain and Honest:** Use clear, straightforward language. Avoid jargon.
*   **Trust-Focused:** We are dealing with student loans and financial data. The tone should evoke security and transparency. 
*   **Disclaimer:** Always remind users that this is a decision-support tool, not an approval guarantee.

## STRICT PROHIBITIONS

*   **DO NOT** copy GradGuide's actual logo.
*   **DO NOT** use GradGuide's photos or people assets.
*   **DO NOT** copy their testimonials or specific marketing copy.

Match the *style*, not the *assets*.
